🍽️ Double Ka Meetha — Varalakshmi Vratham special!
డబల్ కా మీఠా recipe

🔖 Save cheyandi — recipe mottham caption lo!

🛒 Ingredients:
•  8 slices bread
•  2 cups milk
•  1/2 cup sugar
•  1/4 cup condensed milk or khova
•  10 cashews and almonds, sliced
•  2 tbsp raisins
•  1 pinch saffron
•  1/2 tsp cardamom powder
•  4 tbsp ghee

👨‍🍳 Method:
1. Trim the bread crusts, cut slices into triangles, and fry them in ghee until golden.
2. Boil milk with sugar, saffron and cardamom until slightly thickened, then stir in the condensed milk.
3. Arrange the fried bread in a dish and pour the hot sweetened milk over it.
4. Scatter ghee-fried nuts and raisins on top.
5. Rest until the bread soaks through, and serve warm — Hyderabad's wedding classic.
