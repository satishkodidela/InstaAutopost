🍽️ Lao Som Pak Pickled Cabbage

🔖 Save cheyandi — recipe mottham caption lo!

🛒 Ingredients:
• 1 large Cabbage
• 10 Spring Onions
• 16 oz Water
• 2 tablespoons Salt

👨‍🍳 Method:
1. Cut the cabbage and chop it into 1 inch pieces
2. Put the cabbage in a large bowl and add cold water to rinse.
3. Wash the cabbage. Rinse and drain three times.
4. In a large clean bowl, pour in the bottle of water.
5. Put the cabbage and green onion into the salt water mixture.
6. Add in the salt.
