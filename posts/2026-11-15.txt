🍽️ Layered mango custard

🔖 Save cheyandi — recipe mottham caption lo!

🛒 Ingredients:
• 1 ½ tbsp Custard
• 3 Cups Whipping Cream
• 3 Cups Milk
• 1/3 cup Sugar
• 6 tablespoons Water
• 1 ½ tbsp Vanilla Extract
• Garnish Mint
• Garnish Mango
• Topping Mango
• 5 tablespoons Water
• To taste Sugar

👨‍🍳 Method:
1. For the pudding custard
2. Combine water and gelatin in a bowl
3. Let it rest for about 7-10 minutes
4. Combine cream, milk and sugar and heat for a few minutes until all the sugar melts. Do not boil.
5. Take it off the stovetop and let it rest for about 5 minutes
6. Add gelatin mixture and stir. Add vanilla essence.
7. Pour mixture into small glasses at an angle. It's really rich so the smaller glasses will help reduce the serving.
8. Transfer to refrigerator to chill for a minimum of 4 hours. ( I kept mine in the refrigerator overnight) Please don't put it in the freezer.
9. For the mango puree
10. When you're ready to add the mango purée to the top of the pudding, you can then start the process of preparing the puree
11. Using a blender or hand help mixer, purée mango chunks/slices
12. Strain purée for a finer consistency
13. You can use puree as is or you can add some sugar for a little more sweetness
14. To make the puree like pudding
15. Add sugar to puree
16. Transfer to a stove top and stir until sugar is dissolved. Remove from stovetop
17. Add gelatin mixture and stir consistently
18. Remove chilled creamy custard from refrigerator and add mango puree mixture to the top of it
19. Chill for a minimum of 2 hours (longer is better)
20. Garnish with mint and small mango pieces and serve
