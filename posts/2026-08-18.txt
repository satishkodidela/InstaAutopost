🍽️ Rava Laddu
రవ్వ లడ్డు recipe

🔖 Save cheyandi — recipe mottham caption lo!

🛒 Ingredients:
•  2 cups fine semolina (rava)
•  1 cup sugar, powdered
•  1/2 cup grated dry coconut
•  10 cashews
•  2 tbsp raisins
•  1/2 tsp cardamom powder
•  1/2 cup ghee
•  1/4 cup warm milk

👨‍🍳 Method:
1. Roast the rava in two spoons of ghee on low heat until aromatic, never browning it.
2. Fry cashews and raisins in ghee until golden.
3. Mix rava, powdered sugar, coconut, cardamom and the fried nuts.
4. Add the remaining warm ghee and sprinkle warm milk little by little.
5. Press and roll into tight laddus while the mixture is still warm.
