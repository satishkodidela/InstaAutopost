🍽️ Bangaladumpa Vepudu
బంగాళదుంప వేపుడు recipe

🔖 Save cheyandi — recipe mottham caption lo!

🛒 Ingredients:
•  4 potatoes, cubed small
•  1 tbsp red chilli powder
•  1 tsp coriander powder
•  1/2 tsp turmeric
•  1 tsp mustard seeds
•  1 tbsp chana dal
•  2 sprigs curry leaves
•  4 tbsp oil
•  salt

👨‍🍳 Method:
1. Crackle mustard seeds and chana dal in hot oil, then add curry leaves.
2. Add the potato cubes and turmeric, and toss to coat.
3. Fry uncovered on medium heat, turning occasionally, until the edges crisp and brown.
4. Sprinkle chilli powder, coriander powder and salt.
5. Toss for two more minutes until every cube is crusty and serve with rice and pappu.
