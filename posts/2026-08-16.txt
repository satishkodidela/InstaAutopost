🍽️ Munakkaya Kura
మునక్కాయ కూర recipe

🔖 Save cheyandi — recipe mottham caption lo!

🛒 Ingredients:
•  3 drumsticks, cut into finger-length pieces
•  1 onion, chopped
•  2 tomatoes, chopped
•  1 tbsp ginger garlic paste
•  1 tbsp red chilli powder
•  1 tsp coriander powder
•  1/2 tsp turmeric
•  2 tbsp grated coconut
•  1 tsp mustard seeds
•  1 sprig curry leaves
•  3 tbsp oil
•  salt

👨‍🍳 Method:
1. Crackle mustard seeds in oil, then saute onion, curry leaves and ginger garlic paste.
2. Add tomatoes, turmeric, chilli powder and coriander powder, and cook to a thick masala.
3. Add the drumstick pieces and salt, and coat them in the masala.
4. Pour a cup of water, cover and simmer until the drumsticks are tender and the gravy thickens.
5. Finish with grated coconut and serve with rice — suck the pulp from the pods!
