🍽️ Pachi Pulusu
పచ్చి పులుసు recipe

🔖 Save cheyandi — recipe mottham caption lo!

🛒 Ingredients:
•  1 lemon-sized ball tamarind
•  1 onion, finely chopped
•  3 green chillies
•  1 tsp red chilli powder
•  1 tbsp jaggery
•  2 tbsp coriander leaves
•  1 tsp mustard seeds
•  1 tsp cumin seeds
•  2 dried red chillies
•  1 sprig curry leaves
•  2 tbsp oil
•  salt

👨‍🍳 Method:
1. Soak tamarind in warm water and squeeze out a thin extract into a wide bowl.
2. Roast the green chillies directly on the flame, crush them, and add to the tamarind water.
3. Add chopped onion, jaggery, chilli powder, salt and coriander — this pulusu is never cooked.
4. Crackle mustard, cumin, red chillies and curry leaves in hot oil.
5. Pour the hot tempering into the raw pulusu — it sizzles loudly — and serve with hot rice.
