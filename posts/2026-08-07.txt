🍽️ Jantikalu — Varalakshmi Vratham special!
జంతికలు recipe

🔖 Save cheyandi — recipe mottham caption lo!

🛒 Ingredients:
•  3 cups rice flour
•  1/2 cup besan (gram flour)
•  1 tbsp sesame seeds
•  1 tsp carom seeds (vaamu)
•  1 tsp red chilli powder
•  3 tbsp hot oil, for the dough
•  oil for deep frying
•  salt

👨‍🍳 Method:
1. Mix rice flour, besan, sesame, carom, chilli powder and salt; rub in the hot oil.
2. Knead with water into a smooth, pliable dough.
3. Fill the jantikalu press and squeeze spirals directly into hot oil.
4. Fry until the hissing stops and the spirals turn light gold.
5. Drain, cool and break into crunchy pieces — the everyday Telugu tea-time murukulu.
