🍽️ Chicken 65
చికెన్ 65 recipe

🔖 Save cheyandi — recipe mottham caption lo!

🛒 Ingredients:
•  500g boneless chicken, bite-sized
•  1 tbsp ginger garlic paste
•  1 tbsp red chilli powder
•  1/2 tsp turmeric
•  2 tbsp corn flour
•  2 tbsp rice flour
•  1 egg
•  1/2 cup curd
•  6 green chillies, slit
•  2 sprigs curry leaves
•  oil for deep frying
•  salt

👨‍🍳 Method:
1. Marinate chicken with ginger garlic paste, chilli powder, turmeric, salt, egg and the flours for 30 minutes.
2. Deep fry in hot oil in small batches until deep red and crisp; drain.
3. Whisk curd with a pinch of chilli powder and salt.
4. In a hot pan with a spoon of oil, crackle curry leaves and green chillies, add the curd and let it sizzle dry.
5. Toss the fried chicken in this coating until glossy and serve hot with onion rings.
