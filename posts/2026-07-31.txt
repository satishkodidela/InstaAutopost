🍽️ Kothimeera Pachadi
కొత్తిమీర పచ్చడి recipe

🔖 Save cheyandi — recipe mottham caption lo!

🛒 Ingredients:
•  2 cups coriander leaves, packed
•  4 green chillies
•  1 tbsp urad dal
•  1 small ball tamarind
•  1 tsp mustard seeds
•  2 dried red chillies
•  1 sprig curry leaves
•  3 tbsp oil
•  salt

👨‍🍳 Method:
1. Fry urad dal and green chillies in a little oil until the dal turns golden.
2. Add the coriander leaves and saute just until they wilt.
3. Grind with tamarind and salt into a bright green, thick pachadi.
4. Crackle mustard seeds, red chillies and curry leaves in oil.
5. Pour the tempering on top and serve with rice, dosa or upma.
