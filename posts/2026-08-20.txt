బగారా అన్నం recipe

🔖 Save cheyandi — recipe mottham caption lo!

🛒 Ingredients:
•  2 cups basmati rice
•  1 onion, sliced
•  3 green chillies, slit
•  1 tbsp ginger garlic paste
•  4 cloves
•  2 cardamom
•  1 stick cinnamon
•  1 bay leaf
•  2 tbsp mint leaves
•  3 tbsp ghee
•  salt

👨‍🍳 Method:
1. Heat ghee and fry cloves, cardamom, cinnamon and bay leaf until fragrant.
2. Add sliced onion, green chillies and ginger garlic paste; saute until golden.
3. Add soaked and drained basmati rice and gently fry for two minutes.
4. Pour hot water, add mint and salt, and cook covered on low heat until done.
5. Fluff with a fork and serve with any Hyderabadi curry or dalcha.
