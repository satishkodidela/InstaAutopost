ఉత్తప్పం recipe

🔖 Save cheyandi — recipe mottham caption lo!

🛒 Ingredients:
•  3 cups dosa batter (slightly thick)
•  1 onion, finely chopped
•  1 tomato, finely chopped
•  2 green chillies, chopped
•  2 tbsp coriander leaves
•  1 tsp grated carrot (optional)
•  3 tbsp oil
•  salt

👨‍🍳 Method:
1. Mix chopped onion, tomato, green chillies and coriander in a bowl.
2. Pour a thick ladle of batter on a hot tawa and spread gently into a small, thick circle.
3. Sprinkle the vegetable topping generously over the wet batter and press lightly.
4. Drizzle oil around, cover and cook until the base is golden and the top is set.
5. Flip for a minute to sear the topping, then serve hot with chutney.
