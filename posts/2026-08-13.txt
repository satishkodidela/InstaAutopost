🍽️ Paramannam
పరమాన్నం recipe

🔖 Save cheyandi — recipe mottham caption lo!

🛒 Ingredients:
•  1 cup rice
•  1 litre milk
•  1 cup jaggery, grated
•  10 cashews
•  2 tbsp raisins
•  1/2 tsp cardamom powder
•  3 tbsp ghee

👨‍🍳 Method:
1. Cook the rice in milk on low heat, stirring, until completely soft and creamy.
2. Melt the jaggery with a spoon of water and strain the syrup.
3. Take the rice off the boil and stir in the jaggery syrup — off heat, so the milk never splits.
4. Fry cashews and raisins in ghee and fold in with cardamom.
5. Serve warm as naivedyam — the first sweet made for every Telugu festival.
