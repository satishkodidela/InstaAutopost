🍽️ Pongali
పొంగలి recipe

🔖 Save cheyandi — recipe mottham caption lo!

🛒 Ingredients:
•  1 cup rice
•  1/2 cup moong dal
•  1 tsp black pepper, coarsely crushed
•  1 tsp cumin seeds
•  1 tbsp grated ginger
•  2 tbsp cashews
•  1 sprig curry leaves
•  4 tbsp ghee
•  salt

👨‍🍳 Method:
1. Dry roast moong dal until aromatic, then wash it with the rice.
2. Pressure cook rice and dal together with extra water until completely soft and mushy.
3. Heat ghee and fry cashews, crushed pepper, cumin, ginger and curry leaves.
4. Pour the sizzling tempering over the cooked rice-dal mash and add salt.
5. Mix well, mash once more with the ladle, and serve hot with coconut chutney.
