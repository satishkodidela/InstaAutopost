🍽️ Mutton Kura — Varalakshmi Vratham special!
మటన్ కూర recipe

🔖 Save cheyandi — recipe mottham caption lo!

🛒 Ingredients:
•  750g mutton, curry cut
•  2 onions, finely chopped
•  2 tomatoes, chopped
•  2 tbsp ginger garlic paste
•  2 tbsp red chilli powder
•  1 tbsp coriander powder
•  1 tsp garam masala
•  1/2 tsp turmeric
•  1/2 cup curd
•  2 sprigs curry leaves
•  4 tbsp oil
•  salt

👨‍🍳 Method:
1. Marinate the mutton with curd, turmeric and half the ginger garlic paste for one hour.
2. Brown the onions well in oil, then add remaining ginger garlic paste and curry leaves.
3. Add tomatoes and the spice powders, and cook until the masala releases oil.
4. Add the mutton and sear on high heat, then pressure cook with a cup of water until fork-tender.
5. Simmer open until the gravy thickens and coats the meat; finish with garam masala.
