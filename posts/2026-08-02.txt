🛒 Ingredients:
•  2 cups rice, soaked overnight
•  1.5 cups jaggery, grated
•  1 tbsp sesame seeds
•  2 tbsp ghee
•  oil for frying

👨‍🍳 Method:
1. Drain the soaked rice, shade-dry briefly, and pound or grind into a fine moist flour.
2. Melt jaggery with a little water and boil to a soft-ball syrup.
3. Stir the rice flour into the syrup off the heat to form a soft, glossy dough.
4. Pat small discs on a greased leaf or sheet, pressing sesame seeds into each.
5. Fry gently in ghee and oil, pressing with ladles, until deep brown; drain and cool.
