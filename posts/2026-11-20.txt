🍽️ Coq au vin

🔖 Save cheyandi — recipe mottham caption lo!

🛒 Ingredients:
• 1½ tbsp Olive Oil
• 3 rashers (100g) chopped dry-cured Bacon
• 12 small Shallots
• 2 (460g) Chicken Legs
• 4 (650g) Chicken Thighs
• 2 (280g) Chicken Breasts
• 3 finely chopped Garlic
• 3 tbsp Brandy
• 600ml Red Wine
• 150ml Chicken Stock
• 2 tsp tomato puree
• 3 sprigs thyme
• 2 sprigs Rosemary
• 2 bay leaves
• garnish parsley
• 250g chestnut mushroom
• 2 tbsp plain flour
• 1 tsp butter

👨‍🍳 Method:
1. Heat 1 tbsp of the oil in a large, heavy-based saucepan or flameproof dish. Tip in the bacon and fry until crisp. Remove and drain on kitchen paper.
2. Add the shallots to the pan and fry, stirring or shaking the pan often, for 5-8 mins until well browned all over. Remove and set aside with the bacon.
3. Pat the chicken pieces dry with kitchen paper. Pour the remaining oil into the pan, then fry half the chicken pieces, turning regularly, for 5-8 mins until well browned. Remove, then repeat with the remaining chicken. Remove and set aside.
4. Scatter in the garlic and fry briefly, then, with the heat medium-high, pour in the brandy or Cognac, stirring the bottom of the pan to deglaze. The alcohol should sizzle and start to evaporate so there is not much left.
5. Return the chicken legs and thighs to the pan along with any juices, then pour in a little of the wine, stirring the bottom of the pan again.
6. Stir in the rest of the wine, the stock and tomato purée, drop in the bouquet garni, season with pepper and a pinch of salt, then return the bacon and shallots to the pan.
7. Cover, lower the heat to a gentle simmer, add the chicken breasts and cook for 50 mins-1hr.
