🍽️ Bendakaya Vepudu
బెండకాయ వేపుడు recipe

🔖 Save cheyandi — recipe mottham caption lo!

🛒 Ingredients:
•  500g okra (bendakaya), cut into rounds
•  1 onion, sliced
•  1 tbsp red chilli powder
•  1 tsp coriander powder
•  1/2 tsp turmeric
•  1 tsp mustard seeds
•  1 tbsp chana dal
•  1 sprig curry leaves
•  4 tbsp oil
•  salt

👨‍🍳 Method:
1. Wash and completely dry the okra before cutting so it never turns slimy.
2. Crackle mustard seeds and chana dal in hot oil with curry leaves.
3. Add the okra and fry uncovered on medium-high heat, stirring only occasionally.
4. When the edges crisp, add sliced onion, turmeric and salt and fry until the onion browns.
5. Finish with chilli powder and coriander powder, toss two minutes and serve.
