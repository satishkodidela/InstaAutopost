🍽️ Chepala Vepudu
చేపల వేపుడు recipe

🔖 Save cheyandi — recipe mottham caption lo!

🛒 Ingredients:
•  500g fish steaks (seer or tilapia)
•  2 tbsp red chilli powder
•  1 tbsp ginger garlic paste
•  1/2 tsp turmeric
•  1 tbsp rice flour
•  1 lemon
•  2 sprigs curry leaves
•  6 tbsp oil
•  salt

👨‍🍳 Method:
1. Pat the fish dry and slash the flesh lightly so the masala goes deep.
2. Rub with chilli powder, ginger garlic paste, turmeric, salt, lemon juice and rice flour; rest 30 minutes.
3. Heat oil in a flat pan and lay the fish in a single layer.
4. Shallow fry undisturbed until the underside is crisp and red, then flip once.
5. Crisp the other side with curry leaves in the oil and serve with onion and lemon.
