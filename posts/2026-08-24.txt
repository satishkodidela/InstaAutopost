🍽️ Palakova
పాలకోవా recipe

🔖 Save cheyandi — recipe mottham caption lo!

🛒 Ingredients:
•  2 litres full-fat milk
•  3/4 cup sugar
•  2 tbsp ghee
•  1/2 tsp cardamom powder

👨‍🍳 Method:
1. Boil the milk in a wide, heavy kadai on medium heat.
2. Stir continuously, scraping the sides and bottom, as the milk reduces and thickens.
3. When it reaches a thick, grainy consistency, add the sugar — it loosens again.
4. Keep stirring until the kova leaves the sides of the kadai in one mass.
5. Finish with ghee and cardamom, cool slightly and serve — the famous milk sweet of Andhra.
