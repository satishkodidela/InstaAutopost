🍽️ Beerakaya Kura
బీరకాయ కూర recipe

🔖 Save cheyandi — recipe mottham caption lo!

🛒 Ingredients:
•  500g ridge gourd, peeled and cubed
•  1 onion, chopped
•  1 tomato, chopped
•  3 green chillies, slit
•  1/2 cup milk or 2 tbsp grated coconut
•  1/2 tsp turmeric
•  1 tsp mustard seeds
•  1 tsp cumin seeds
•  1 sprig curry leaves
•  3 tbsp oil
•  salt

👨‍🍳 Method:
1. Crackle mustard and cumin in oil, then saute onion, green chillies and curry leaves.
2. Add tomato and turmeric and cook until soft.
3. Add the ridge gourd cubes and salt, cover and cook — the gourd releases its own sweet water.
4. When tender, stir in milk or coconut and simmer two minutes for a silky gravy.
5. Serve with rice or hot chapatis.
