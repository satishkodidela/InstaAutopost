🍽️ Ullipaya Pakodi
ఉల్లిపాయ పకోడి recipe

🔖 Save cheyandi — recipe mottham caption lo!

🛒 Ingredients:
•  3 onions, thinly sliced
•  1.5 cups besan (gram flour)
•  3 tbsp rice flour
•  4 green chillies, chopped
•  1 tsp red chilli powder
•  1 sprig curry leaves, chopped
•  2 tbsp hot oil, for the batter
•  oil for deep frying
•  salt

👨‍🍳 Method:
1. Squeeze the sliced onions with salt until they wilt and release moisture.
2. Add besan, rice flour, chillies, chilli powder, curry leaves and hot oil — bind using only the onion juices.
3. Sprinkle water just if needed; the mix should stay ragged, not a batter.
4. Drop rough clusters into hot oil and fry until deep golden and crisp.
5. Drain and serve immediately with tea — crunchiest while hot.
