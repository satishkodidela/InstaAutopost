🍽️ Aloo Kurma
ఆలూ కుర్మా recipe

🔖 Save cheyandi — recipe mottham caption lo!

🛒 Ingredients:
•  4 potatoes, cubed
•  1 onion, chopped
•  2 tomatoes, pureed
•  1 tbsp ginger garlic paste
•  1/2 cup grated coconut
•  1 tbsp poppy seeds (gasagasalu)
•  6 cashews
•  1 tsp garam masala
•  1 tbsp red chilli powder
•  1/2 tsp turmeric
•  3 tbsp oil
•  salt

👨‍🍳 Method:
1. Grind coconut, poppy seeds and cashews to a smooth paste.
2. Saute onion and ginger garlic paste in oil until golden, then add tomato puree and spice powders.
3. Add the potato cubes and salt, and coat in the masala.
4. Pour in the ground paste with a cup of water and simmer until the potatoes are soft and the kurma is creamy.
5. Serve with poori, chapati or bagara annam.
