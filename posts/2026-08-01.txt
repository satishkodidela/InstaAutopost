🍽️ Potlakaya Vepudu
పొట్లకాయ వేపుడు recipe

🔖 Save cheyandi — recipe mottham caption lo!

🛒 Ingredients:
•  500g snake gourd, sliced thin
•  1 onion, chopped
•  1 tbsp red chilli powder
•  1/2 tsp turmeric
•  2 tbsp grated coconut
•  1 tsp mustard seeds
•  1 tbsp chana dal
•  1 sprig curry leaves
•  3 tbsp oil
•  salt

👨‍🍳 Method:
1. Crackle mustard seeds and chana dal in oil, then add curry leaves and onion.
2. Add the snake gourd slices and turmeric, and saute on medium heat.
3. Cook uncovered, stirring now and then, until the gourd softens and browns lightly.
4. Season with chilli powder and salt, and finish with grated coconut.
5. Toss once and serve with rice and rasam.
