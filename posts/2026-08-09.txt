🍽️ Dibba Rotti
దిబ్బ రొట్టె recipe

🔖 Save cheyandi — recipe mottham caption lo!

🛒 Ingredients:
•  2 cups idli rava
•  1 cup urad dal
•  1 tsp cumin seeds
•  2 green chillies, chopped
•  1 sprig curry leaves
•  4 tbsp oil
•  salt

👨‍🍳 Method:
1. Soak urad dal for 4 hours and grind to a thick, fluffy batter.
2. Mix in washed idli rava, cumin, green chillies, curry leaves and salt; rest 15 minutes.
3. Heat oil in a deep, heavy kadai and pour in the thick batter.
4. Cover and cook on the lowest flame until the bottom forms a deep golden, thick crust.
5. Flip once, cook briefly, and serve wedges hot with avakaya or ginger chutney.
