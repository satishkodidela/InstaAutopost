🍽️ Honey Cookies

🔖 Save cheyandi — recipe mottham caption lo!

🛒 Ingredients:
• 50g Honey
• 300ml Rye Flour
• 100ml Water

👨‍🍳 Method:
1. Dissolve honey into water
2. Add flour. Let set a few minutes. Knead the mixture for a short time. Using a teaspoon, form the dough into round buns/cakes and place on the greased or baking paper-lined oven plate
3. Bake at 200 C for 10-15 minutes
