🍽️ Chamadumpa Vepudu
చామదుంపల వేపుడు recipe

🔖 Save cheyandi — recipe mottham caption lo!

🛒 Ingredients:
•  500g taro root (chamadumpa)
•  1 tbsp red chilli powder
•  1 tsp coriander powder
•  1/2 tsp turmeric
•  1 tsp mustard seeds
•  1 tbsp urad dal
•  2 sprigs curry leaves
•  5 tbsp oil
•  salt

👨‍🍳 Method:
1. Boil the taro roots until just tender, cool, peel and slice into thick rounds.
2. Crackle mustard seeds and urad dal in hot oil with curry leaves.
3. Slide in the taro slices in a single layer and fry undisturbed until golden.
4. Turn and crisp the other side, then sprinkle turmeric, chilli powder, coriander powder and salt.
5. Fry until the edges crackle — crisp outside, soft inside — and serve hot.
