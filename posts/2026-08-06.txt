🍽️ Hyderabadi Veg Biryani — Varalakshmi Vratham special!
హైదరాబాదీ వెజ్ బిర్యానీ recipe

🔖 Save cheyandi — recipe mottham caption lo!

🛒 Ingredients:
•  2 cups basmati rice
•  300g mixed vegetables (carrot, beans, potato, cauliflower)
•  1 cup curd
•  2 onions, thinly sliced
•  2 tbsp ginger garlic paste
•  2 tbsp biryani masala
•  1/2 cup mint and coriander leaves
•  1 pinch saffron soaked in milk
•  4 tbsp ghee
•  salt

👨‍🍳 Method:
1. Marinate the vegetables in curd, ginger garlic paste, biryani masala and salt for 30 minutes.
2. Fry the sliced onions until golden and crisp; boil soaked rice until 70 percent done.
3. Layer marinated vegetables, rice, fried onions, mint and saffron milk in a heavy pot.
4. Seal the lid and cook on dum on the lowest flame for 25 minutes.
5. Fluff gently and serve hot with raita.
