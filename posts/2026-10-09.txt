🛒 Ingredients:
• 2 cups Sweetcorn
• 1/3 cup Flour
• 1/4 cup Sugar
• 1/3 cup Milk
• 2 Eggs
• 2 tablespoons Butter
• 1 tsp Vanilla
• 1/4 tsp Cinnamon
• Pinch Salt

👨‍🍳 Method:
1. Using a food processor or a blender, combine all the ingredients, don't overdo it, the mixture should be crumby.
2. ​
3. Heat a lightly oiled pan over medium-high heat. Pour 1/4 cup of the batter onto the pan. Cook until lightly brown on each side, just like making pancakes ;)
4. Traditionally you serve it with a spoon of sour cream on top. I just prefer to eat it plain.
