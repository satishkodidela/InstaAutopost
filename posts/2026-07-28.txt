🍽️ Mamidikaya Pulihora
మామిడికాయ పులిహోర recipe

🔖 Save cheyandi — recipe mottham caption lo!

🛒 Ingredients:
•  2 cups cooked rice, cooled
•  1 cup grated raw mango
•  1 tsp mustard seeds
•  1 tbsp chana dal
•  1 tbsp urad dal
•  3 tbsp peanuts
•  4 green chillies, slit
•  2 dried red chillies
•  1/2 tsp turmeric
•  2 sprigs curry leaves
•  4 tbsp oil
•  salt

👨‍🍳 Method:
1. Crackle mustard seeds in oil, then fry chana dal, urad dal and peanuts until golden.
2. Add green chillies, red chillies, curry leaves and turmeric to the tempering.
3. Add the grated raw mango and saute for two minutes so it stays tangy but loses rawness.
4. Spread the cooled rice in a wide vessel, pour the mango tempering over it and add salt.
5. Mix gently with fingers or a flat ladle without mashing the rice and rest 10 minutes before serving.
