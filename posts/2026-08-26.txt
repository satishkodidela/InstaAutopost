🍽️ Kodi Guddu Pulusu
కోడిగుడ్డు పులుసు recipe

🔖 Save cheyandi — recipe mottham caption lo!

🛒 Ingredients:
•  6 eggs, hard boiled
•  2 onions, sliced
•  2 tomatoes, chopped
•  1 lemon-sized ball tamarind
•  1 tbsp ginger garlic paste
•  1 tbsp red chilli powder
•  1 tsp coriander powder
•  1/2 tsp turmeric
•  1 tsp mustard seeds
•  2 sprigs curry leaves
•  3 tbsp oil
•  salt

👨‍🍳 Method:
1. Score the boiled eggs lightly and fry them in a little oil until golden blisters form; set aside.
2. Crackle mustard seeds, then saute onions, curry leaves and ginger garlic paste until golden.
3. Add tomatoes and the spice powders and cook to a soft masala.
4. Pour in tamarind water and salt, and simmer until the pulusu thickens and turns glossy.
5. Slide in the fried eggs, simmer five minutes so they drink the gravy, and serve with rice.
