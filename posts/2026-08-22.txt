🍽️ Poornam Boorelu
పూర్ణం బూరెలు recipe

🔖 Save cheyandi — recipe mottham caption lo!

🛒 Ingredients:
•  1 cup chana dal
•  1 cup jaggery, grated
•  1/2 tsp cardamom powder
•  1 cup urad dal
•  1 cup rice
•  oil for deep frying
•  1 pinch salt

👨‍🍳 Method:
1. Soak rice and urad dal for four hours and grind to a thick dosa-like batter with a pinch of salt.
2. Pressure cook chana dal until just soft, drain fully, and mash with jaggery over low heat into a thick poornam.
3. Add cardamom and roll the poornam into small balls once cool.
4. Dip each ball into the batter to coat completely.
5. Deep fry until golden brown all over and serve warm — crisp shell, molten sweet dal inside.
