🛒 Ingredients:
•  2 cups rice flour
•  1 tbsp moong dal, soaked
•  1 tbsp sesame seeds
•  1 tsp cumin seeds
•  1 tsp red chilli powder
•  2 tbsp butter
•  oil for deep frying
•  salt

👨‍🍳 Method:
1. Boil a cup of water with salt and butter, then stir in the rice flour to make a stiff dough.
2. Knead in soaked moong dal, sesame, cumin and chilli powder while warm.
3. Roll small ropes of dough and join the ends into little rings.
4. Deep fry the rings in medium oil until they turn golden and stop sizzling.
5. Cool completely — they crisp as they cool — and store airtight.
