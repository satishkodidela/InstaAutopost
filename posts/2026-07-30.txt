🍽️ Royyala Vepudu
రొయ్యల వేపుడు recipe

🔖 Save cheyandi — recipe mottham caption lo!

🛒 Ingredients:
•  500g prawns, cleaned
•  2 onions, finely chopped
•  1 tbsp ginger garlic paste
•  2 tbsp red chilli powder
•  1 tsp coriander powder
•  1/2 tsp turmeric
•  1 tsp garam masala
•  2 sprigs curry leaves
•  1 lemon
•  4 tbsp oil
•  salt

👨‍🍳 Method:
1. Marinate the prawns with turmeric, salt and a squeeze of lemon for 15 minutes.
2. Saute the onions in oil until golden, then add ginger garlic paste and curry leaves.
3. Add chilli powder and coriander powder, and fry the masala until fragrant.
4. Add the prawns and fry on high heat — they curl and turn pink in minutes; never overcook.
5. Finish with garam masala, toss until the masala clings dry to each prawn, and serve hot.
