🍽️ Kodi Kura
కోడి కూర recipe

🔖 Save cheyandi — recipe mottham caption lo!

🛒 Ingredients:
•  750g chicken
•  2 onions, finely chopped
•  2 tomatoes, chopped
•  2 tbsp ginger garlic paste
•  2 tbsp red chilli powder
•  1 tbsp coriander powder
•  1 tsp garam masala
•  1/2 tsp turmeric
•  2 tbsp grated coconut, ground to paste
•  2 sprigs curry leaves
•  4 tbsp oil
•  salt

👨‍🍳 Method:
1. Saute onions in oil until deep golden, then add ginger garlic paste and curry leaves.
2. Add tomatoes, turmeric, chilli powder and coriander powder and cook to a glossy masala.
3. Add the chicken and salt, and sear on high heat until the masala clings to every piece.
4. Add the coconut paste with a cup of hot water, cover and simmer until the chicken is tender.
5. Finish with garam masala and simmer until the oil floats on the gravy; serve with rice or roti.
