🍽️ Masala Dosa
మసాలా దోస recipe

🔖 Save cheyandi — recipe mottham caption lo!

🛒 Ingredients:
•  3 cups dosa batter
•  3 potatoes, boiled
•  1 onion, sliced
•  2 green chillies
•  1 tsp mustard seeds
•  1 tsp chana dal
•  1/2 tsp turmeric
•  1 sprig curry leaves
•  3 tbsp oil
•  2 tbsp ghee
•  salt

👨‍🍳 Method:
1. Crackle mustard seeds and chana dal in oil, add curry leaves, green chillies and sliced onion.
2. Add turmeric, mashed boiled potatoes and salt; mix into a soft masala and set aside.
3. Spread a ladle of dosa batter thin on a hot tawa and drizzle ghee around the edges.
4. Cook until golden and crisp, place a scoop of potato masala in the centre.
5. Fold the dosa over the masala and serve hot with chutney and sambar.
