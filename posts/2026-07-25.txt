🍽️ Hyderabadi Chicken Biryani
హైదరాబాదీ చికెన్ బిర్యానీ recipe

🔖 Save cheyandi — recipe mottham caption lo!

🛒 Ingredients:
•  500g chicken
•  2 cups basmati rice
•  1 cup curd
•  2 onions, thinly sliced
•  2 tbsp ginger garlic paste
•  2 tbsp biryani masala
•  1 tsp red chilli powder
•  1/2 cup mint and coriander leaves
•  4 green chillies, slit
•  1 pinch saffron soaked in milk
•  4 tbsp ghee
•  salt

👨‍🍳 Method:
1. Marinate chicken with curd, ginger garlic paste, biryani masala, chilli powder, salt and half the mint for 1 hour.
2. Deep fry the sliced onions until deep golden and crisp.
3. Boil soaked basmati rice with whole spices and salt until 70 percent cooked, then drain.
4. Layer the marinated chicken, half-cooked rice, fried onions, mint and saffron milk in a heavy pot.
5. Seal and cook on dum: high heat for 5 minutes, then lowest flame for 25 minutes.
6. Rest 10 minutes, open, fluff gently and serve with raita and mirchi ka salan.
