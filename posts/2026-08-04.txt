కొబ్బరి అన్నం recipe

🔖 Save cheyandi — recipe mottham caption lo!

🛒 Ingredients:
•  2 cups cooked rice, cooled
•  1 cup fresh grated coconut
•  1 tsp mustard seeds
•  1 tbsp chana dal
•  1 tbsp urad dal
•  3 tbsp cashews
•  3 green chillies, slit
•  1 dried red chilli
•  2 sprigs curry leaves
•  3 tbsp ghee
•  salt

👨‍🍳 Method:
1. Heat ghee and fry cashews until golden; set aside.
2. Crackle mustard seeds, then fry chana dal, urad dal, chillies and curry leaves.
3. Add the grated coconut and saute gently until it smells sweet, without browning.
4. Add the cooled rice, salt and fried cashews.
5. Toss gently until every grain is coated white and serve warm.
