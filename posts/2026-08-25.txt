🍽️ Tomato Pachadi
టమాటా పచ్చడి recipe

🔖 Save cheyandi — recipe mottham caption lo!

🛒 Ingredients:
•  6 ripe tomatoes, chopped
•  6 dried red chillies
•  1 tbsp urad dal
•  1 tsp mustard seeds
•  1 small ball tamarind
•  3 garlic cloves
•  1 sprig curry leaves
•  4 tbsp oil
•  salt

👨‍🍳 Method:
1. Fry urad dal and red chillies in oil until golden and set aside.
2. In the same oil, cook the tomatoes with tamarind and salt until soft and jammy.
3. Cool and grind everything coarsely with garlic — keep some texture.
4. Crackle mustard seeds and curry leaves in a spoon of oil.
5. Pour the tempering over the pachadi and serve with hot rice, idli or dosa.
