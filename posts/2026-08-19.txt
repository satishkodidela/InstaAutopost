🍽️ Masala Vada
మసాలా వడ recipe

🔖 Save cheyandi — recipe mottham caption lo!

🛒 Ingredients:
•  2 cups chana dal
•  2 onions, finely chopped
•  4 green chillies, chopped
•  1 tbsp ginger, chopped
•  1 tsp fennel seeds
•  2 sprigs curry leaves, chopped
•  3 tbsp coriander leaves
•  oil for deep frying
•  salt

👨‍🍳 Method:
1. Soak chana dal for two hours and grind coarsely without water, keeping some dal whole.
2. Mix in onion, green chillies, ginger, fennel, curry leaves, coriander and salt.
3. Shape handfuls into flat patties on your palm.
4. Deep fry in medium-hot oil until deep golden and crunchy.
5. Drain and serve hot with tea — crisp edges, soft centre.
