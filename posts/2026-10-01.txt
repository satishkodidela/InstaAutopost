🍽️ Apple pie pops

🛒 Ingredients:
• 1 tablespoon Sugar
• 1 1/2 cups All purpose flour
• 1 tsp Salt
• 4 oz Unsalted Butter
• 1 tablespoon Shortening
• 1/4 cup Cold Water
• 1 lb Apples
• 1/4 cup Brown Sugar
• 1 tsp Cinnamon
• 3  tablespoons Unsalted Butter
• 1/2 tsp Ground Nutmeg
• 1/2 tsp Salt
• 3/4 teaspoon Lemon Juice
• Topping Sugar
• 1 Egg
• 2 tsp Water

👨‍🍳 Method:
1. Need Lollipop sticks
2. Instructions for crust
3. Combine the flour, sugar, and salt to a food processor bowl. Mix using the pulse setting.
4. Add butter and shortening and pulse about 10 -12 times until the texture is changed to small pebbles.
5. Add ¾ of the ice water and pulse a few more times until the dough takes the shape of a ball. Add more water as needed. Don't overmix. Don't worry if the dough isn't completely blended. You will use your rolling pin to finish creating the crust.
6. Place dough ball onto the counter and flatten into a flat disc. Wrap it in plastic wrap and refrigerate for at least an hour or overnight.
7. Instructions for pie filling
8. Heat butter over medium heat, until just golden brown
9. Add brown sugar, spices, salt and lemon juice. Stir to combine ingredients
10. Add diced apples and stir.
11. Allow apples to cook for about 5-7 minutes, over medium heat
12. Remove from heat and let the apple mixture cool. You can place apples in the refrigerator to speed up the process.
13. Mix egg and water for an egg wash.
14. Roll out pie dough to 1/8 inch thickness.
15. Cut out rounds of 2 ½ to 3 inches and place them on parchment paper. You will need to roll out the extra dough left over scraps from cutting.
16. In half of the rounds, cut out shapes or holes for ventilation purposes
17. On the other half of the rounds, gently press one lollipop stick about halfway up each round.
