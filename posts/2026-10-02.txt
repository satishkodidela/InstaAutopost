🍽️ Cassava bread

🔖 Save cheyandi — recipe mottham caption lo!

🛒 Ingredients:
• 3 Cups Farine
• 3 Cups Water
• 1 cup Cassava Flour
• Pinch Salt

👨‍🍳 Method:
1. Mix dry ingredients in a bowl
2. Add water and mix well
3. When mixture has achieved a doughy like consistency, roll into little balls
4. Roll balls with the palm of your hands, and flatten slightly like s disk
5. In a plate, cover balls with the rest of the farine to create a very course texture
6. Place on a hot heavy cast iron griddle pressing down to flatten with and roast on both sides until lightly brown
7. Remove and serve or add your special accompaniment
