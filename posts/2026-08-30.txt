🍽️ Gummadikaya Pulusu
గుమ్మడికాయ పులుసు recipe

🔖 Save cheyandi — recipe mottham caption lo!

🛒 Ingredients:
•  500g yellow pumpkin, cubed
•  1 onion, sliced
•  1 lemon-sized ball tamarind
•  2 tbsp jaggery
•  1 tbsp red chilli powder
•  1/2 tsp turmeric
•  1 tsp mustard seeds
•  1 tsp fenugreek seeds
•  2 sprigs curry leaves
•  3 tbsp oil
•  salt

👨‍🍳 Method:
1. Crackle mustard and fenugreek seeds in oil, then saute the onion with curry leaves.
2. Add pumpkin cubes, turmeric and salt, and fry for a few minutes.
3. Pour in tamarind water and simmer until the pumpkin is just tender.
4. Add jaggery and chilli powder, and simmer until the pulusu turns glossy — sweet, sour and hot in one spoon.
5. Serve with hot rice.
