🍽️ Ulava Charu
ఉలవ చారు recipe

🔖 Save cheyandi — recipe mottham caption lo!

🛒 Ingredients:
•  1 cup horse gram (ulavalu)
•  1 onion, chopped
•  2 tomatoes, chopped
•  1 lemon-sized ball tamarind
•  2 tbsp red chilli powder
•  1 tsp coriander powder
•  1 tsp mustard seeds
•  1 tsp cumin seeds
•  2 sprigs curry leaves
•  3 tbsp oil
•  2 tbsp fresh cream (optional)
•  salt

👨‍🍳 Method:
1. Soak horse gram overnight and pressure cook until very soft.
2. Strain and reserve the dark cooking stock; grind a little of the cooked gram and add back to thicken.
3. Simmer the stock with tamarind water, chilli powder, coriander powder and salt until it thickens to a glossy dark charu.
4. Crackle mustard, cumin and curry leaves in oil with onion and tomato, and add to the simmering charu.
5. Reduce until spoon-coating thick and serve with hot rice and a swirl of cream.
